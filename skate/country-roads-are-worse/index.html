Almost everyone pictures this the wrong way round: quiet country lanes good, busy streets bad. On wheels it inverts, and skaters who ride both will tell you so.

Rural surfaces are the problem. Small holes, patched tarmac, edges that have crumbled away — any one of them stops a wheel dead while the rest of you keeps going. City pavement is mostly smooth for an unglamorous reason: somebody maintains it.

So the danger in town is the traffic, and the danger in the countryside is the ground. Those are not equivalent risks, because the ground is what actually puts skaters down.

It changes what you look at when you plan a route. Not how quiet it is. What it is made of, and whether anyone has repaired it lately.
