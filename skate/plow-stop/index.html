The Plow Stop: The Easiest Stop to Learn

Written 7 August 2026 · updated 10 November 2026 · by Cass · written with an AI assistant

The plow stop is the easiest stop to learn: roll with feet a little wider than your hips, push your heels apart so the toes turn in, bend your knees and press down and outward through the whole foot. Both feet stay down, so no single-leg balance is needed. It is the most useful stop long term.

Why learn the plow stop first?

The plow is the first stop to learn because it works on quads and inlines, needs no special hardware, and keeps both feet on the ground.

How do you do a plow stop on roller skates?

Roll with feet a little wider than your hips. Push your heels apart so the toes turn in, then press down and outward through the whole foot. The friction does the work — do not try to force the skates together.

How fast should you practise the plow stop?

At walking pace, on a flat surface, first. Speed makes it easier, not harder, but only once the shape is automatic.
