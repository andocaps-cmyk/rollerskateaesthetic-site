Can you put skate liners in the washing machine?

If the liners come out of your boots, they can go in the washing machine. Cold water, gentle cycle. That part surprises people, and it is the easy half.

Can you tumble-dry skate liners?

No. They air-dry, and that is not optional. Heat destroys the foam. A liner that has been through a tumble dryer comes out shrunken and hard, it never gets its shape back, and no amount of wearing it in will bring it back.

Why is a heat-damaged liner such a problem?

It is the only skate-care mistake on this whole site that you cannot undo. A wheel wears out and you replace it. A bearing gets filthy and you clean it. A cooked liner is simply finished, and it usually takes the fit of the boot with it.

What if the liner does not come out?

Do not try to force it. Lift the insole out instead, dry that on its own, and open the boot up so air moves through the rest.
