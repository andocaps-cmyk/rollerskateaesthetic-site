Going Faster Makes the One-Foot Turn Easier

Why is a one-foot turn easier at speed?

It is the same reason a bicycle is easier to ride at speed than walked. Rolling carries balance with it. Crawl into the turn and you are asking a single leg to hold you up while almost nothing is helping — every wobble has time to grow into a fall.

What does speed ask of your body?

The catch is everything above your knees, and it is a real catch. More speed asks for a more solid body: arms held out to either side, shoulder blades pulled together, stomach engaged, and no swinging about looking for balance mid-turn.

In what order should you practise it?

Get the shape right standing still, then at a walking roll, then let the speed come. Speed does not fix a loose body; it makes a loose body worse and a solid one better.
