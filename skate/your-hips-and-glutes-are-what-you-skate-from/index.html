The parts of the body a beginner thinks about are the feet and the knees. A certified coach gives the hips their own off-skate work, and the reason is what they do for the rest of the leg.

Why do hips and glutes matter in skating?

Strengthening the muscles around the hips, all of the glutes above all, leaves a skater feeling more supported, more balanced and more in control on the wheels. That is her description of the effect, and it is the effect a beginner is chasing with every drill.

Why does my lower back feel stiff after skating?

The hips are closely tied to how the lower back feels after a skate. A skater who finishes sessions stiff through the back often has hips that are doing less than their share.

When should you train your hips for skating?

Between sessions, not during one: this is preparation, not a fix. It is done in shoes, on a floor, on the days the skates stay in the bag, and it shows up later as a skater who feels held up from the middle.
