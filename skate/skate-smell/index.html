What makes roller skates smell?

Moisture and the bacteria living in it, not grime. Skate smell reads like dirt, so people treat it like dirt. That is the mistake — and once you know which one it is, the fix changes.

Why does deodorising spray not fix skate smell?

Because it only masks the symptom for a session. The cause is still sitting in the foam, wet, doing what it was doing before. It comes back, and it comes back faster each time, which is how people conclude that their skates are just like that.

How do you get rid of skate smell?

Dry first, properly — laces loose, tongue forward, air moving through the whole boot, out of the bag. Then, if there is anything left to treat, treat it. The order matters more than the product.

Doing it the other way round is not a small inefficiency. It is the entire reason the problem keeps coming back, and it is why the answer to a smelly boot is usually a habit rather than a purchase.
