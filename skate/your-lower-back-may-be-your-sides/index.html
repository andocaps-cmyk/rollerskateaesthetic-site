A Stiff Lower Back After Skating May Be Your Sides

Written 17 September 2026 · updated 10 November 2026 · by Cass · written with an AI assistant

A stiff lower back after skating may come from your sides, not your back. The place that aches is not always the place at fault, and a stiff lower back after a long skate is one of the clearest examples of it.

Why does my lower back hurt after skating?

A certified coach points at the side body — the muscles running down your flanks, hard to reach and easy to ignore — and says that weakness or tightness there can contribute to lower back pain.

What should you stretch after a long skate?

Possibly not your back. If the sides are involved, the useful work after a long session may not be aimed at your back at all, and the back stretches you already do may be treating a symptom.

Is this a diagnosis of back pain?

No. It is a coach's observation across a lot of skaters, not a diagnosis of your back. A pain that keeps coming back, or that arrives without skating, is a question for someone qualified to answer it.
