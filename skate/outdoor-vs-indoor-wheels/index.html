What do the numbers on skate wheels mean?

They are the hardness. Wheel hardness is printed on the wheel as a number followed by A. Higher is harder. Rink wheels sit around 95A and up because a polished floor is already smooth and a hard wheel rolls faster on it.

Why do indoor wheels feel awful outdoors?

Pavement has grain, and a hard wheel transmits every bit of it into your feet. Outdoor wheels of 78A to 85A deform slightly and absorb it, which is why the same skates feel like different skates once you swap.

Do you need new skates to skate outside?

No. A set of soft wheels costs less than a month of rink entry, and it fits the skates you already own. This is the cheapest upgrade in skating and almost nobody mentions it to beginners.
