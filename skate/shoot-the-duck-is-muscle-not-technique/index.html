Shoot the Duck Is Muscle, Not Technique

The squat with one leg stretched out in front of you — English speakers usually call it shoot the duck — looks like it must contain a trick. It does not.

How do you learn to shoot the duck on skates?

By repetition: it is quadriceps. There is no cue you are missing and no secret about weight placement that unlocks it. The people who can hold that position are the people who have squatted a few hundred times, and the learning is a repetition, not an insight.

What if you still cannot shoot the duck?

You do not go looking for a better explanation. You go and do more of them. There is no cue you are missing; the learning is a repetition, not an insight.

What is shoot the duck called in French?

French skaters have not even settled on a name for it. Depending on who taught you it is the théière, the cafetière, the pistolet or the bonne femme — teapot, coffee pot, pistol, old dear. Four names, one move, and nobody ever agreed.
