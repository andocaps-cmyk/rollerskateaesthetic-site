Skates are built to be rolled on wet roads. Getting them wet is not the mistake, and it is not something you can realistically avoid anyway — roads are wet sometimes.

Zipping them straight into a bag afterwards is the mistake. Sealed in plastic, the water has nowhere to go. It sits against every metal part for hours, and that is what rusts: the bearings, the axles, the hardware.

The fix is not a maintenance routine. Leave them out until they are dry before they go anywhere. A wipe if they are soaked, then air and a bit of patience.

It is the same logic as wet shoes, and it is worth separating from deeper skate cleaning, which is its own subject with its own answers.
