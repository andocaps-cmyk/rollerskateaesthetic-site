Roller Skate Size Calculator

Written 7 October 2026 · updated 17 November 2026 · by Cass · written with an AI assistant

How to size roller skates: measure your foot heel to longest toe, standing, in millimetres, at the end of the day. Then read the skate brand's own size chart, not your street-shoe size. Each brand sizes differently, so follow the advice the brand gives for its own skates.

Once you have the right size, fasten laces and straps firmly — a skate you can move in is a skate you can't steer.

How do you measure your foot for roller skates?

Stand on a sheet of paper against a wall, heel touching the wall. Mark the tip of your longest toe, then measure heel to mark in millimetres. Do it late in the day, when your foot is at its widest, with the socks you skate in. Measure both feet and use the longer one. Most pain beginners blame on skating (blisters, numb toes, heel lift) is actually a sizing problem.

Why does roller skate size depend on the brand?

Brands do not agree: Impala says take your usual size and go up a full size between half sizes, Rio Roller says its sizes often run a little big, and Powerslide asks you to add 3 to 5 mm to your foot length. That is why this calculator uses each brand's published chart and shows its own advice.

FAQ

Should I go up a size in roller skates?

It depends on the brand, so follow its own advice. Impala, for example, recommends your usual size and the next full size up if you are between half sizes. Powerslide and Chaya ask you to add a few millimetres to your measured foot length before reading the chart.

Are roller skates the same size as shoes?

No. A skate size is not a shoe size and it varies a lot between makers. Always check the chart of the brand you are buying.

What if I have wide feet?

Look at the width of the model, not only its length: choose a model with a wider last or width options (Powerslide lists five widths; Chaya describes its truFIT last as medium to wide in the front). Check the model page.

Does it work for kids?

Yes, where the brand publishes kids' sizes: Riedell (youth and junior), Rollerblade (Apex adjustable), Bont (sizes 9C to 13.5C), Roller Derby and Moxi (US kids' column), and Crazy Skates (junior sizes). Measure a child's foot the same way and pick “Foot length”, or their US kids' shoe size.
