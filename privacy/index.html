Privacy Policy

Written 7 August 2026 · updated 20 November 2026 · by Cass · written with an AI assistant

What this site collects, what it does not, and how the Pinterest API is used.

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Roller Skate Aesthetic is a small independent publication about roller skating, operated by one person. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to data in connection with rollerskateaesthetic.com and with the Pinterest account @rollerskateaesthetic.

What this website collects

Nothing, unless you choose to join the email list. This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, has no login, no comment form and no advertising scripts. We do not build profiles of visitors. The only personal data we receive is the email address you enter yourself in the sign-up form described in the next section.

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and collects no personal data. We only see aggregate figures, such as visits per page and the site a visitor came from.

Two ordinary technical exceptions apply, and they are outside our control:

  • Hosting logs. The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes connection data such as IP address, user agent and requested URL in order to deliver the page and protect the service against abuse. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Web fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the request in order to serve the font file. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Email sign-up (newsletter)

Some pages carry a form to receive an email when we publish new roller skating guides. Using it is optional; nothing on this site requires it.

  • Who collects it. The sign-up form and the emails are run by MailerLite, our email service provider. When a page with the form loads, your browser fetches the form from MailerLite’s servers. Roller Skate Aesthetic decides why the address is used; MailerLite processes it on our behalf.
  • What is collected. Your email address, and nothing else. We do not ask for your name.
  • Why. Only to send you the Roller Skate Aesthetic newsletter: announcements of new guides. We do not sell or rent the list, and we do not use it for anything else.
  • Legal basis. Your consent. The consent box in the form is never ticked for you; you must tick it yourself.
  • Double opt-in. After you submit the form, you receive an email with a confirmation link. You are added to the list only once you click it. If you never click it, the address is not subscribed and no newsletter is sent.
  • Unsubscribing. Every email contains an unsubscribe link. One click removes you from the list. You can also write to us at the address below.
  • How long we keep it. For as long as you stay subscribed. When you unsubscribe, or ask us to delete it, the address is removed from the active list; we keep only the minimum record of your consent and your unsubscription that we need to show we respected your choice.
  • Where. MailerLite may process data outside your country, under the safeguards described in its own privacy policy.

Use of the Pinterest API

This is the section relevant to Pinterest's developer review.

We use the Pinterest API v5 for a single, narrow purpose: to publish our own content to our own Pinterest account. The application has one user — the owner of this site. It is not offered to the public, it has no other users, and it does not act on behalf of anyone else.

  • Authorisation. Access is obtained exclusively through Pinterest's standard OAuth 2.0 flow. We never ask for, collect, or store Pinterest login credentials, passwords or session cookies.
  • Scopes. We request only what publishing requires — writing Pins, and reading the account's own boards and Pin analytics.
  • Tokens. The OAuth access token and refresh token are held privately by the account owner, are never transmitted to any third party, and are never exposed on this website. They can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account settings.
  • Data retrieved. Only data belonging to our own account — our boards, our Pins and their performance figures. We do not retrieve, store or process data about other Pinterest users.
  • Data sent. Only content we authored ourselves: images, titles, descriptions and destination links for our own Pins.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links on this site are affiliate links. If you follow one and make a purchase, the merchant may pay us a small commission at no additional cost to you. Those merchants set their own cookies and operate under their own privacy policies once you leave this site. Affiliate links are disclosed on the pages where they appear and on any Pin that carries one.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Your rights

If you have signed up to the email list, the only personal data we hold about you is your email address. Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask us to give you access to it, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or give you a copy; you can withdraw your consent at any time (the unsubscribe link in every email does this). Write to us at the address below and we will reply. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For visitors who have not signed up, we hold no personal data, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes to how the Pinterest integration works will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, the email list, or the Pinterest integration, and requests about your data: contact@rollerskateaesthetic.com.
