What each skater says

None of the nine compares the two brands directly. Five lists are WFTDA “featured skater” gear lists from 2010 to 2016; the four RollerBones voices all have a commercial tie to the wheel they ride. Read it as “who rides what”, not as a verdict.

Radar

  • Bonnie Thunders (jammer often called the greatest player of modern derby; World Cups 2011, 2014, 2018, 2025; 8 WFTDA titles) rode Radar Blue Zodiac in 2010 (gear list, WFTDA, Aug 2010). Today she has a RollerBones signature wheel, see below.
  • Snot Rocket Science (Team USA 2011 and 2014) listed Radar Halo among her wheels, alongside Bont FXX and Ballistic; no sponsor stated (gear list, WFTDA, 4 Oct 2016).
  • Juniper Simonis (WFTDA champion 2015 with Windy City) rode Radar Presto; no sponsor stated (gear list, WFTDA, 10 Jul 2016).
  • Nina Erwes (Helsinki All-Stars captain for four years, MVP Big O 2016) rode Radar Presto on custom Wikko skates (gear list, WFTDA, 8 Sep 2016).
  • Jackie Daniels (Windy City Rollers, skater in the film Whip It, coach) rode Radar Devil Ray 95A and Speed Ray 93A, so 93-95A (gear list, WFTDA, 1 Mar 2010).

RollerBones

  • Bonnie Thunders now has a RollerBones signature wheel: 62 x 38 mm, 94A or 96A, aluminium hub. The figures come from the product, not from her own words (distributor listing, undated).
  • Estro Jen (Michelle Steilen, founder of Moxi Skates; signature RollerBones Bowl Bombers) tells park beginners to ride 92A or harder and would pick 62 mm over 57 mm for a beginner, for speed with less effort; she adds that her talk is “not an advertisement for my wheels”, though it is her own wheel (Estro Jen, 9 Jan 2021; Moxi, 31 Jan 2021).
  • Caro Hernandez (Colombian bowl skater, ex-derby; Moxi, Powerdyne, RollerBones and S1 gear) rode RollerBones Estro Jen 62 mm in 2021 and Elite 103A narrow wheels for sliding in 2020 (S1 blog, 6 Dec 2021; Moxi, 7 Oct 2020).
  • Kid Ace (Moxi team rider: vert, gaps, stairs; record not verified) rides RollerBones Bowl Bombers 101A for speed (Moxi, 25 Sep 2020).

What the split really is

In our sources the brand matters less than the hardness and the floor. The Radar voices are derby players on indoor floors at 93-95A; the RollerBones voices are mostly park skaters at 101-103A, plus Thunders’ 94-96A derby wheel. Scald Eagle, who rides neither, says to choose a wheel by hardness, not colour (Bont blog, 14 Sep 2020, a brand text).

Which one for a beginner

No skater in our compilation names either brand for a first wheel. What the sources do give: Estro Jen’s 92A floor and 62 mm for a park beginner; Caro’s view that soft, gummy wheels are “amazing” for a beginner on the street and on rough floors (Moxi, 7 Oct 2020). So: pick the hardness for your floor first, then either brand in that hardness.

From the skater behind this site

  • 78A-85A outside, 95A and up on a smooth rink floor. A smooth floor lets a hard wheel roll fast; grainy asphalt sends every bump through a hard wheel into your feet, a softer one absorbs it. The skater behind this site learned on quads but has skated inline for most of his life and does not skate quads now, so this is general, not brand advice.

Beginner FAQ

Are Radar or RollerBones wheels better?

Our sources do not say: none of the nine skaters compares them. Five derby players listed Radar (Thunders in 2010, Snot Rocket Science, Juniper Simonis, Nina Erwes, Jackie Daniels); four skaters with a RollerBones tie ride RollerBones (Thunders today, Estro Jen, Caro Hernandez, Kid Ace). Hardness for your floor matters more.

What hardness do derby players ride?

93-95A for Jackie Daniels (Radar), 94-96A for Bonnie Thunders' RollerBones signature wheel. Scald Eagle says to choose by hardness, not colour.

What hardness for the skatepark?

Estro Jen says 92A or harder from the first session; the park pros in our sources ride 101-103A RollerBones.

Which size for a beginner, 57 or 62 mm?

Estro Jen would pick 62 mm for a park beginner: more speed for less effort. 57 mm gives more feel once you can pump.

Do the skaters get paid to ride these wheels?

The RollerBones riders are all tied to the brand (signature wheels or a Moxi team setup). The five Radar riders state no sponsor in their WFTDA gear lists.
