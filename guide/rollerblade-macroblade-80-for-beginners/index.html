Questions

Is the Macroblade 80 good for beginners?

It meets the points the coaches in our compilation give for a first inline skate: a supportive cuff (Asha, Shulgan, Welch), four 80 mm wheels at 82A (Welch, Shulgan, Thisissoul) and a metal frame (Thisissoul). It only partly meets Welch's and Casper's wish for a frame that takes other wheels: Rollerblade says up to 84 mm. No pro skater we compiled recommends it by name.

Macroblade 80 or Zetrablade?

Both have four 80 mm 82A wheels and a supportive cuff. The Macroblade 80 has an aluminium frame and takes 84 mm wheels; the Zetrablade has a one-piece composite frame built for 80 mm.

Can I put bigger wheels on the Macroblade 80?

Rollerblade says it is upgradable to 84 mm wheels. It does not say it takes larger ones.
