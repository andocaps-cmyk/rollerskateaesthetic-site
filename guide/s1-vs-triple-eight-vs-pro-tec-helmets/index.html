What people who skated them report

These are the reports we could find and read from people who say they actually used them, with their limits. We did not use Amazon customer reviews, which Amazon does not allow sites to reproduce.

  • Triple Eight Certified Sweatsaver Helmet: Neil Hester, a skateboarder (not a roller skater) who reviews his own gear on YouTube, had worn one for at least six months and bought a second (video from about 2024). He found the fit very snug once he picked the right size from his head circumference, with a spare set of thicker pads in the box to adjust it, and calls it the most comfortable helmet he has tried. The catch is the “sweat saver” brow pad: for a skater who sweats a lot it soaks up sweat and then drips into the eyes, so he wrings it out during sessions; the side pads go in the washing machine, but he advises against machine-washing the centre pad, whose fabric came unstuck. After several falls where his head hit the ground, the shell showed no damage. (source, watched 24 Oct 2026)

What each skater wears

This comparison is lopsided and we say so: Triple Eight and Pro-Tec each have a single voice, both from WFTDA gear lists of 2010. S1 has seven, but part of that count comes from S1’s own blog. Nobody compares two helmets in our sources.

S1

  • Scald Eagle (Rose City and Team USA jammer, WFTDA champion 2015 and 2016) wore an S1 helmet; Bont appears as her skate brand (WFTDA gear list, 1 Oct 2015).
  • Snot Rocket Science (Team USA 2011 and 2014) wore the S1 Lifer; no sponsor stated (WFTDA gear list, 4 Oct 2016).
  • Satan’s Little Helper (Raquel Davila, three straight titles with the Arizona Derby Dames, international coach) wore an S-One helmet (WFTDA gear list, 10 Jun 2016).
  • Nina Erwes (Helsinki All-Stars captain, MVP Big O 2016) wore S1 (WFTDA gear list, 8 Sep 2016).
  • Caro Hernandez (Colombian bowl skater; S1 among her sponsors) wears the S1 Lifer (S1 blog, 6 Dec 2021).
  • Tia Pitman (“Steeez T”, winner of Concrete Waves 2023; Chuffed and S1 rider) wears the S1 Lifer (S1 blog, 3 Nov 2023).
  • Slam (founder of Chuffed Skates; brand voice, no verified competition record) recommends S1 and asks for a certified hard-shell helmet with adjustable pads (Chuffed, 18 Aug 2022).

Triple Eight

  • Bonnie Thunders (often called the greatest player of modern derby; 8 WFTDA titles, four World Cups) wore a Triple 8 helmet in 2010 (WFTDA gear list, Aug 2010). A WFTDA video of her on helmet fitting exists (WFTDA, 12 Jul 2020), but we could not read its transcript, so nothing from it is used here.

Pro-Tec

  • Jackie Daniels (Windy City Rollers, skater in Whip It, coach) wore a Pro-Tec Ace with Triple Eight pads (WFTDA gear list, 1 Mar 2010).

Which one for a beginner

No skater in our sources tells a beginner which brand to buy. What they agree on is the type: a hard-shell helmet, worn from the first session. Slam asks for a certified one; Estro Jen ranks the helmet first in her list of park gear (Estro Jen, 9 Jan 2021). No skater mentions a safety standard or when to replace a helmet after a crash: check the certification printed on the listing and the box yourself, and fit by head circumference.

From the skater behind this site

Beginner FAQ

Which helmet do top skaters wear most?

In our compilation, S1: seven skaters wear or recommend it (Scald Eagle, Snot Rocket Science, Satan's Little Helper, Nina Erwes, Caro Hernandez, Tia Pitman, Slam). Triple Eight and Pro-Tec have one voice each (Bonnie Thunders and Jackie Daniels, both in 2010).

Is S1 better than Triple Eight or Pro-Tec?

Our sources do not say. No skater compares helmets, two S1 voices are S1 riders featured on S1's blog, and the Triple Eight and Pro-Tec voices are single 2010 gear lists.

What helmet should a beginner buy?

A certified hard-shell helmet that fits your head circumference, worn from the first session. Slam asks for a certified one; Estro Jen puts the helmet first in her park gear list.

Is the Pro-Tec Classic the same as the Pro-Tec Ace?

No. Jackie Daniels wore the Ace; the Classic is the Pro-Tec model we could check on Amazon, and its listing showed no certification.

When should I replace my helmet?

No skater in our compilation says. Follow the maker's instructions printed with the helmet.
