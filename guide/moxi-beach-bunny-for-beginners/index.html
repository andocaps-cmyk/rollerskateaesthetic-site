Who it suits, and who should look elsewhere

It suits an adult who will mostly skate outside on paths and smooth streets and wants a quad that is ready to roll out of the box.

Look elsewhere if you will skate mostly on a rink floor (look for harder wheels), or you want a leather boot. If you have not chosen between quad and inline yet, start with quad vs inline: which to buy first.

Questions

Is the Moxi Beach Bunny good for beginners?

It meets several points the quad coaches in our compilation give: an aluminium plate (Lisa J, Dunbeezy), soft 78A wheels for outdoors and an adjustable toe stop (Dirty Deborah Harry). It misses two: a vinyl rather than leather boot (Dunbeezy) and 58 mm wheels where Kelly at Skate Base London advises 62-65 mm. No pro skater we compiled recommends it by name.

Is the Beach Bunny an indoor or an outdoor skate?

Its 78A wheels are soft, which suits outdoor surfaces. Lisa J and Dirty Deborah Harry both say soft wheels outside and harder wheels indoors, so on a rink you would change the wheels.

What size Beach Bunny should I get?

Follow Moxi's size chart. Moxi sells full sizes only, 1 to 10, in medium width.
