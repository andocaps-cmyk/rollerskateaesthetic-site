What people who skated it report

These are the reports we could find and read from people who say they actually skated it, with their limits. We did not use Amazon customer reviews, which Amazon does not allow sites to reproduce.

  • Lucy Ann Brown, YouTube video of 18 Jun 2021, made after 80 days of skating them every day as a complete beginner; bought them herself; the description carries an Amazon affiliate link. Ran true to size (shoe size 6, skate size 6) and comfortable. The boot began to tear around the heel by day 80, and she reports that other owners saw tears within a month. Toe stops bulky but working; the small stock wheels made her feel every bump on trails. Her verdict: good for a beginner who will skate occasionally or for a few months before upgrading, not for skateparks. (source, read 24 Oct 2026)
  • Larissa Cooper (Lissa Skates on Instagram and TikTok), an artistic roller skater, YouTube video of about 2025 comparing the Impala with the Moxi Panther; she says the video is not sponsored, and the description carries purchase links. Weighed both: the Impala was the lighter of the two (3.2 against 3.9 on her scale). She found its boot stiffer, with less ankle flex but more support. The toe stop is not adjustable and cannot take a jam plug, though it will never work loose. Her verdict: either is a good beginner skate for the first year or two. (source, read 24 Oct 2026)
  • A blog post on schimiggy.com signed Alex Tran, undated; the page also offers the author’s own pair of Impalas for sale, which it does not flag as a conflict of interest. Bought and skated the Impala and a Moxi side by side. Impala ran true to size (street size 7, skate size 7); the sole felt stiff and less comfortable than the Moxi; the heel is very high; the wheels handled smooth asphalt and rougher concrete. The author kept the Moxi. (source, read 24 Oct 2026)

Who it suits, and who should look elsewhere

It suits a beginner who wants a light, colourful first quad for occasional skating and does not plan to push it hard.

Look elsewhere if you plan to skate often or progress quickly: the PVC boot and fixed toe stop are what Dunbeezy and Dirty Deborah Harry treat as signs of an entry-level skate. If you have not chosen between quad and inline yet, start with quad vs inline: which to buy first.

Sizing

Impala’s own advice, when we read it on 6 October 2026, was true to size, with men told to go up two sizes from their men’s shoe size because the sizing is women’s. On Zappos, owners’ reviews disagree on whether it runs small or narrow (read 24 Oct 2026). See should you size up.

Questions

Are Impala skates good for beginners?

They meet one point the quad coaches in our compilation give, an aluminium plate (Lisa J, Dunbeezy), and miss three: a PVC rather than leather boot (Dunbeezy), a fixed rather than adjustable toe stop (Dirty Deborah Harry, Dunbeezy) and 58 mm wheels where Kelly at Skate Base London advises 62-65 mm. No pro skater we compiled recommends them by name.

Are Impala wheels good for outdoors?

They are 58 mm and 82A: harder than the soft wheels Lisa J and Dirty Deborah Harry advise for outdoors, softer than rink wheels.

Do Impala skates run small?

Impala told buyers to go true to size, with men going up two sizes, when we read its site on 6 October 2026. Owners on Zappos disagree on whether they run small or narrow.
